Career timeline

  1. Senior Manager - Data Analytics, RadixJuly 2025 - Current · Mumbai, India
    • Led development of competitive SERP intelligence for GoDaddy and Namecheap, integrating scraping, BigQuery transformation, KPI definition, data validation, and Tableau delivery to enhance tracking of TLD appearance share, competitive positioning, keyword performance, and geographic trends.
    • Designed and delivered a standardized Namecheap SERP dashboard with corrected aggregation and denominator logic, enabling consistent appearance-share and positioning analysis across daily, weekly and monthly reporting levels.
    • Led development and refinement of GoDaddy .tech keyword analysis to identify keyword-level competitive visibility, including cases where .tech was absent while competing extensions such as .ai and .io appeared, supporting bidding and TLD-positioning analysis.
    • Expanded GoDaddy scraping and analysis into multiple geographies, collaborating with technology teams to validate data availability, identify gaps, and establish reliable analytical datasets beyond the US market.
    • Built reusable analytical solutions for GoDaddy .com positioning/spin analysis, premium-domain visibility and other competitive-search use cases, reducing the need to rebuild one-off analyses when refreshed data became available.
    • Led cross-functional analytics and decision-support initiatives spanning competitive intelligence, pricing and premium-domain analysis, SERP analytics, channel performance, data quality, market intelligence, scraping infrastructure and AI-enabled automation.
    • Built AI-assisted Python web scrapers for three client data-acquisition workflows and deployed them as locally scheduled cron processes, replacing recurring AI/token-dependent execution with automated operational workflows.
    • Used Codex-assisted development to establish a GoDaddy cart-upsell scraper, solving a data-acquisition challenge affected by Akamai blocking that had prevented successful local implementation through earlier development attempts.

  2. Business Intelligence Manager, RadixJuly 2022 - June 2025 · India
    • Led Business Intelligence initiatives across business, brand, channel, finance and technology teams, translating business questions into analytical workflows, dashboards, automated reporting and decision-support outputs.

    • Designed and validated ETL correction logic addressing erroneous transaction revenue; testing demonstrated a reduction in erroneous revenue proportion from approximately 0.60% to 0.25%, improving downstream data reliability.
    • Built and enhanced SERP/appearance-share reporting across GoDaddy and other registrar/client datasets, identifying historical data gaps, validating reparsed data, coordinating source corrections and improving reporting reliability for business-critical competitive metrics.
    • Automated recurring premium-domain reporting, including domain-status checks, sell-through analysis and incremental NPV summaries, creating reusable processes for time-sensitive pricing and inventory exercises.
    • Mentored analysts through recurring 1:1s, technical learning plans and project guidance, supporting development in Tableau, Python, time management, written stakeholder communication and expansion beyond routine operational responsibilities.
    • Proactively investigated a sharp increase in Stripe payment disputes (September to December 2024); identified patterns, coordinated user blocking and additional account-security measures, and helped reduce monthly dispute cases by approximately 88% (123 in November to 15 in December) and monthly losses by approximately 90%.
    • Identified a complex NCC escrow billing issue across 12 TLDs and drove commercial resolution with the vendor; uncovered substantial historical excess billing and negotiated a revised structure that reduced the annual run-rate by more than 50% versus the 2023 billed cost.
    • Consolidated escrow billing, achieving recurring annual savings, while uncovering invoicing anomalies and coordinating resolution with the provider.
    • Negotiated a WhoisXML premium-plan upgrade during 2023-24, reducing the proposed annual cost by approximately 38% while maintaining access to business-critical registration data.
    • Managed migration/onboarding to an enhanced WhoisXML API providing broader domain-registration datasets, coordinating the transition to preserve data continuity and minimize gaps in recurring analytical processes.
  3. Lead Analyst, RadixJuly 2017 - June 2022 · Mumbai, India

    • Built and maintained business intelligence solutions using SQL, BigQuery, Tableau and Excel, supporting management reporting and recurring business decision-making.
    • Enhanced analytical dashboards and reporting processes, establishing validation practices that improved data consistency and reporting reliability.
